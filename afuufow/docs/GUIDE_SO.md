# Afuufow — hagaha dejinta (Soomaali)

## 1. Rakib
Orod `Afuufow-Setup-x.y.z.exe` (adiga u gaar ah, admin lama rabo). Ka fur **Afuufow** liiska Start. Calaamad ayaa ka muuqanaysa tray-ga; marka daaqadda la xidho Afuufow way sii shaqeysaa. Si aad uga baxdo: tray-ga ama sidebar-ka “Ka bax Afuufow”.

## 2. Dejinta koowaad
1. **Soo dhawow** — dooro luqadda. Akhri xaddidaadaha: waxay shaqeysaa kadib marka aad gasho Windows; ma aha furaha Windows.
2. **Aqoonsi** — magaca buuxa, magaca salaanta, naanaysta.
3. **Adeegyada** — si Soomaali dabiici ah loogu hadlo waxaad u baahan tahay **furaha Azure Speech** (eeg [kharashaadka](HARDWARE_AND_COSTS.md)): ku dheji furaha iyo region-ka, dooro cloud-ka aad oggoshahay, riix *Tijaabi*. AI-ga: rakib **Ollama** oo orod `ollama pull gemma3:4b`, ama oggolow moodel cloud ah.
4. **Moodellada** — soo deji *Silero VAD* iyo moodelka *ereyga toosinta* (≈20 MB, SHA-256 la hubiyo). Ikhtiyaari: moodelka codka, Whisper small.
5. **Codka** — daar ereyga toosinta; riix *Deji erayga* oo dheh “Afuufow” saddex jeer.
6. **Wajiga** (ikhtiyaari) — bilow diiwaangelinta; raac tilmaamaha (si toos ah eeg, madaxa yar u leexi, iftiinka beddel) ilaa 10 muunad la aqbalo.
7. **Codkaaga** (ikhtiyaari) — lix weyn. Cod keliya weligiis ma oggolaado ficillada xasaasiga ah.
8. **Ogolaansho** — deji **PIN**, oggolow Downloads/Documents/Desktop, go’aami weydiinta booqdayaasha.
9. **Isku-xirka** — daar WhatsApp Web oo scan garee QR hal mar; dooro halka YouTube ka ciyaarayo.
10. **Dhammaad** — bilow marka Windows furmo; daar dhegaysiga/kamaradda hadda ama dambe.

## 3. Xiriiriyaasha WhatsApp
*Milkiilaha & Xusuusta → Xiriiriyaasha*: magac, naanaysyo (tusaale “Axmed”), lambar caalami ah oo aan “+” lahayn (tusaale 252611234567), calaamadi *Fariimo ha loo diri karo*. Fariimaha waxaa la diraa kaliya xiriiriyaasha la oggolaaday.

## 4. Amarrada maalinlaha ah
| Dheh / qor | Natiijo |
|---|---|
| “Afuufow, Chrome fur” · “Downloads-ka fur” | wuu furaa |
| “Ii raadi file-ka invoice-ka” | wuxuu ka raadiyaa goobaha la oggolaaday |
| “Google-ka ka raadi cimilada” | raadin webka |
| “Gal YouTube oo ii shid heesta …” | wuxuu ku shidaa daaqadda Afuufow; waa la xaqiijiyaa |
| “Jooji muuqaalka” · “Sii wad” · “Codka hoos u dhig” | hakin · sii wad · codka |
| “Ahmed u dir fariintan: …” | wuu diraa |

Ku hadal (push-to-talk): **Ctrl+Alt+Space** (mar riix bilow, mar kale riix dir). Sanduuqa qoraalka ee Guriga had iyo jeer wuu shaqeeyaa.

## 5. Marka uusan hubin
Su’aal gaaban ayuu weydiiyaa (Axmed kee? muuqaal kee?), wuxuu sheegaa marka aan la xaqiijin karin ficilka, weligiisna kuma diro qof aan la hubin.

## 6. Cilad-bixin
*Cilad-bixin → Orod hubinta* waxay tijaabisaa maykarafoonka, kamaradda, ogolaanshaha, furayaasha, internetka, Ollama, moodellada iyo WhatsApp, waxayna sharaxdaa xalka. Log-ka: *Fur faylasha log-ka*.
